## Current Projects
We proudly work with collaborators from a range of academic, governmental and research institutions, including from the University of Washington.
Below is a non-exhaustive list of some of our presently funded work. 

- [Geodesy and Geophysics in Support of the ICESat-2 Mission](https://app.dimensions.ai/details/grant/grant.14954082)
- [Ice Sheet Mass Balance Inter-Comparison Exercise (IMBIE)](https://app.dimensions.ai/details/grant/grant.14035146)
- [Investigating Antarctic grounding zones using ICESat-2](https://app.dimensions.ai/details/grant/grant.13212262)
- [Reconstruction and Evaluation of Polar Ice Mass Change](https://app.dimensions.ai/details/grant/grant.14046727)
- [Advanced Information Systems to Fill STV Gaps: Next-generation Stereo+lidar Fusion and Sensor Technology](https://app.dimensions.ai/details/grant/grant.13212928) (co-I)
- [Data Fusion for Mass-change Measurements over Complex Ice Surfaces](https://app.dimensions.ai/details/grant/grant.13212941) (co-I)
- [ICESat-2 SlideRule Mission Extension: Enabling scalable, on-demand fusion of altimetry and raster data in the cloud](https://app.dimensions.ai/details/grant/grant.13214194) (co-I)

## Software

We help build software for the scientific community.
Our software is openly developed on GitHub under both the [pyTMD](https://github.com/pyTMD) and [polargeodesy](https://github.com/polargeodesy) organizations, as well as the individual accounts of our [lab team members](../people/index.html).

{{< details title="pyTMD" closed="true" >}}
Python-based tidal prediction software for ocean and Earth tides  
[GitHub](https://github.com/pyTMD/pyTMD) &middot;
[Documentation](https://pyTMD.readthedocs.io) &middot;
[JOSS](https://doi.org/10.21105/joss.08566) &middot;
[PyPI](https://pypi.python.org/pypi/pyTMD/) &middot;
[conda-forge](https://anaconda.org/conda-forge/pytmd)
{{< /details >}}

{{< details title="gravity-toolkit" closed="true" >}}

[GitHub](https://github.com/polargeodesy/gravity-toolkit) &middot;
[Documentation](https://gravity-toolkit.readthedocs.io) &middot;
[PyPI](https://pypi.python.org/pypi/gravity-toolkit/) &middot;
[conda-forge](https://anaconda.org/conda-forge/gravity-toolkit)
{{< /details >}}
