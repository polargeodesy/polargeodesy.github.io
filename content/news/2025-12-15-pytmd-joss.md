---
title: "pyTMD in JOSS"
date: 2025-12-15
summary: "Our paper describing pyTMD was accepted and published in the Journal of Open Source Software"
---

Our [manuscript](https://doi.org/10.21105/joss.08566) describing [pyTMD](https://github.com/pyTMD/pyTMD), a Python-based tidal prediction software, was accepted today!
Our paper gives a foundational overview of the capabilities of the software package, and its relation to other tide prediction software.
pyTMD has been a work in progress for over 9 years, and started as part of an NPP fellowship at NASA GSFC.
After acceptance in JOSS, we released pyTMD version 3.0 that integrates with xarray for data handling.

JOSS is a no-cost open access journal for research software packages where all reviews and comments are performed openly on GitHub.
Our reviewers helped evaluate not just the manuscript, but also the codebase, [documentation](https://pytmd.readthedocs.io/), data structure, and continuous integration testing.
It really was a completely different way of writing and publishing.
